"Typ" as a German Model Code In German automotive terminology, "Typ" literally means "Type". Many famous high-performance and luxury brands use this to designate specific generations or internal factory codes. Porsche: Uses "Type" numbers to track every project. For instance, the Type 901 was the original internal name for the 911. Other famous ones include the Type 64, considered one of the earliest precursors to the brand's sports cars. Audi & Volkswagen: Historically used "Typ" for model generations, such as the Audi RS 6 Typ 4F or the Volkswagen Typ 82 (the wartime Kübelwagen). Auto Union: The legendary V16 Auto Union Typ C was a 1930s racing machine that some consider a "proto-supercar" for its mid-engine layout

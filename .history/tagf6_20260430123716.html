Characterized by eye-watering speeds, aggressive aerodynamics, and often a mid-engine layout, these vehicles are built to deliver an visceral driving experience that few other machines can match. In 2026, the definition of a supercar has evolved to include sophisticated hybrid powertrains—like those found in the Ferrari F80 or the Lamborghini Revuelto—which pair traditional internal combustion roar with instant electric torque.
